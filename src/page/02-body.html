Pass and play · 2 players
Hiding: Drag or tap anywhere and your pug follows your finger around the whole house — just keep walking through doorways from room to room. Touch furniture and your pug walks over — confirm in the corner to hide there.

Seeking: Walk the platypus around the house the same way and touch furniture to check it.

Platytag (multiplayer): One player is the platypus, everyone else is a pug. Pugs get a 15-second head start. The platypus tags pugs by touching them, or by checking the furniture a pug is hiding in. Tagged pugs become platypuses and join the hunt until every pug is caught.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space/Enter to confirm, F to dig, B for the blanket.
